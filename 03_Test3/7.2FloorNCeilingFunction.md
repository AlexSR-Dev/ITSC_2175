7.2 Floor and Ceiling Functions

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


1. Floor Function
Notation: ⌊ x ⌋
DEF: The greatest integer less than or equal to x.
⌊x⌋ <= x < ⌊x⌋ + 1

RECOGNIZE: Floor brackets.
REMEMBER: The result is always an integer.
APPLY: Move downward to the greatest integer that does not exceed the input.

EX:
⌊3.8⌋ = 3
⌊-3.2⌋ = -4
⌊5⌋ = 5


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


2. Ceiling Function
Notation: ⌈ x ⌉
DEF: The smallest integer less than or equal to x.
⌈x⌉ - 1 < x <= ⌈x⌉

RECOGNIZE: Ceiling brackets.
REMEMBER: The result is always an integer.
APPLY: Move upward to the smallest integer that is not less than the input.

EX:
⌈3.2⌉ = 4
⌈-3.8⌉ = -3
⌈5⌉ = 5

3. Participation Activity 7.2.1 - Computing the floor and ceiling functions.

Expression	    Reasoning	                Answer
⌊1.8⌋	        Greatest integer <= 1.8	        1
⌊-1.3⌋	        Greatest integer <= -1.3	   -2

⌈-0.8⌉	        Smallest integer >= -0.8	    0

5. Participation Activity 7.2.3 - Floor and Ceiling Function applied
Q1) A soccer team has x players. A pack of water contains six bottles of water. How many packs of water are needed to provide each player 
with one bottle of water?

APPROACH:
1) Each player needs one bottle.
2) There are x bottles needed.
3) Each pack contains 6 bottles.
4) Divide x by 6.
5) Round up because a partial pack cannot supply a complete pack of six bottles.

RESULT: ⌈x/6⌉


Q2) 
A farmer's hens lay x eggs. How many cartons of eggs can the farmer sell if each carton must contain a dozen eggs?

APPROACH:
1) The farmer has x eggs.
2) Each carton requires 12 eggs.
3) Divide x by 12.
4) Round down because incomplete cartons cannot be sold as full cartons.

RESULT: ⌊x/12⌋
