7.5 - Composition of Functions

GOAL:
- Calculate the composition of two or more functions at a specific value.
- Determine the domain, target, and range of the composition of two or more functions.
- Understand the relationship between a bijection, its inverse, and the identify function.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


1. Definition of Composition of Functions
- f and g are two functions.
WHERE: f : X -> Y AND g : Y -> Z

- The composition of g with f, denoted g o f, is the function:
(g o f) : X -> Z
- Such that for all x ∈ X, (g o f)(x) = g(f(x)).

EX: (g o f)(x)
f(x) = 2x + 3, g(x) = 3x +2

(g o f)(x) = g(f(x)) [DEF]
f(x) = 2x + 3 [Apply f]

2. 7.5.1 Composition of Functions
X = {1,2,3}
Y = {a,b,c}
Z = {7,10,12}

f : X -> Y
f = {(1,a),(2,a),(3,b)}

g : Y -> Z
g = {(a,10),(b,10),(c,12)}

g o f : X -> Z
STEP 1: Element 1
f : 1 -> a
g : a -> 10

THUS: g o f : X -> Z
g o f : 1 -> 10
